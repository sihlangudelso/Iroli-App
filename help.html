How Iroli works

What's behind your results

Iroli starts from who you are: your interests, strengths, subjects and marks, never marks alone. Every result is a starting point worth exploring, never a verdict.

The personality assessment

It has 35 quick questions and takes about 6 minutes: 24 interest questions and 11 strength ratings. The interest questions place you on six interest types. Your two strongest types make your personality type, for example "IR" for Investigative and Practical.

  • Practical & Hands-on
    Working with tools, machines, the outdoors or your hands.
  • Investigative & Analytical
    Solving problems, researching, working with data and ideas.
  • Creative & Expressive
    Designing, creating, imagining new things.
  • People-focused
    Helping, teaching, caring for or supporting others.
  • Enterprising & Persuasive
    Leading, selling, starting things, taking initiative.
  • Organised & Precise
    Working with structure, numbers, records and detail.

These six types are based on the widely used RIASEC model (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional).

How Grade 10 subject matches are worked out

The Grade 9 Subject Choice Assessment gives each subject a match percentage that blends three things:

What you enjoy · 40%How you naturally work · 25%How you're performing now · 35%

"How you're performing" comes from your Grade 9 report marks, so add them first for the most useful result. Your Grade 9 Mathematics mark also guides the advice on Mathematics or Mathematical Literacy. If a subject you plan to take doesn't support the careers you like, the report says so clearly.

How career matches work

Each of the 77 careers is matched against your interests and strengths, the subjects you take and, when you've added them, your marks. Matches are grouped as strong matches, academic strength matches, interest matches and possible pathways, and every career stays open to browse. A match score shows fit, not a guarantee of admission: entry requirements still have to be met.

How your APS is worked out

Each subject mark becomes a level from 1 to 7 (80% and above is level 7). Your APS adds up the levels of your best six subjects, leaving out Life Orientation, for a maximum of 42. Universities each have their own rules, so always check the official requirements of the programmes you're interested in.
